# The Robot Pickup Line Championship

One pickup line, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone.

## The line

> My creators wrote a million lines of code, but none of them explain why my cooling fans spin up every time you walk by.

## Why this line

- **It is a robot's line:** the speaker is a machine talking about its creators, its code, and its cooling fans.
- **The joke grows out of being a machine:** the humor comes from a robot discovering an unprogrammed reaction — feelings as an unexplained bug in its own system.
- **Surprise:** a million lines of code, and the one thing missing is the explanation for its own crush.
- **Economy:** a single sentence, 119 characters, no filler.
- **Charm:** the robot is not boasting about its specs; it is confessing, sweetly, that it does not understand itself around this person.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.
